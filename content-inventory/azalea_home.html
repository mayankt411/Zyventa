Our Brands

Leading Brands in Pharmaceutical Care

Faith Group

Driven by a vision to make quality healthcare accessible, Faith Healthcare Group has invested in infrastructure, technology, and talent to ensure every patient interaction reflects care and professionalism. With strategically located pharmacies and clinics, we provide prescription services, chronic care support, preventive solutions, and wellness products to meet the needs of modern families. Our pharmacists go beyond transactions, offering guidance and reassurance to every individual.

Faith Nutritions

Faith Nutritions is a trusted UAE supplier of premium sports nutrition and vitamins. We offer certified products in bulk, competitive pricing, and fast delivery, serving gyms, retailers, and distributors. Committed to quality and reliability, we make sourcing sports nutrition and wellness supplements seamless and efficient.

Visit Website

Acorus Pharma

Acorus Pharma specializes in supplying, storing, and distributing medical products, skincare, and supplements. We ensure the right products reach patients safely and efficiently, offering pharmacy support, e-commerce, loyalty programs, home delivery, and comprehensive solutions in pharma distribution, logistics, inventory, and regulatory compliance.

Visit Website

Med7 Online

Med7 Online, the digital arm of Faith Healthcare Group, connects 110+ pharmacies to your doorstep across the UAE. As a leading online pharmacy in Dubai, we deliver authentic medicines, wellness products, supplements, and essentials with 45-minute delivery across the UAE. With trusted professionals, exclusive offers, and fast service, quality healthcare is always within reach.

Visit Website

Med7 Clinic

Med7 Clinic, a division of Faith Healthcare Group, has been redefining healthcare in the UAE since 2018. From its first clinic in Dubai, it has grown to 10+ clinics across the emirates. Focused on accessible, patient-centered care and modern treatments, Med7 Clinic combines ethical practice with continuous improvement, strengthening its role as a trusted healthcare partner in the community.

Visit Website

Med7 Pharmacy

Med7 Pharmacy, a division of Faith Healthcare Group, began in 2001 with Faith Pharmacy in Naif, Dubai. What started as a single community outlet has grown into one of the UAE’s fastest-growing retail pharmacy chains. Today, with 110+ pharmacies across the UAE, Med7 delivers accessible, high-quality healthcare, prescription services, and wellness solutions to communities every day.
